# 🎓 StudyFlow: ISP565/ITS665 Data Mining Success Guide

Welcome to your semester roadmap. This guide synthesizes your **Group Project requirements** with **cutting-edge research strategies** to ensure your project isn't just "completed," but stands out as high-impact academic work.

---

### 🚀 TL;DR: Executive Summary
You are tasked with a semester-long end-to-end data mining experiment using **WEKA**. 
*   **The Goal:** Perform a full pipeline (Acquisition $\rightarrow$ Cleaning $\rightarrow$ Transformation $\rightarrow$ Reduction $\rightarrow$ Evaluation) on a dataset of 3,000+ instances.
*   **The X-Factor:** Elevate your project by contextualizing your research within modern trends like **Data-Centric AI, Privacy-Preserving Mining, or Automated Machine Learning (AutoML)**. 
*   **The Key:** Precision in file versioning and clear, comparative analysis between your "processed" and "reduced" datasets.

---

### ✅ The Action Plan
Use this checklist to track your progress throughout the semester:

#### **Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 7–8)**
- [ ] **Form your team (5 members):** Establish clear communication channels.
- [ ] **Select a Dataset:** Must have 3,000+ instances, 20–30 attributes (mixed numeric/nominal).
- [ ] **Research Phase:** Define your "New Frontier" theme (e.g., Explainable AI or Graph Mining) using the search methodologies below.
- [ ] **Approval:** Submit your dataset and topic to the lecturer for sign-off.

#### **Phase 2: The WEKA Pipeline (Weeks 8–12)**
- [ ] **A1: Data Acquisition:** Verify data integrity.
- [ ] **A2: Cleaning:** Handle noise, missing values, and outliers $\rightarrow$ `file-cleaned.arff`.
- [ ] **A3: Transformation:** Perform normalization/discretization $\rightarrow$ `file_processed.arff`.
- [ ] **A4: Reduction:** Execute feature selection $\rightarrow$ `file_reduced.arff`.
- [ ] **B: Model Development:** Run cross-validation (10/20 folds) and % splits (70/30, 80/20). Generate your tree visualizer.

#### **Phase 3: Final Delivery (Weeks 13–14)**
- [ ] **Analysis:** Generate comparative graphs in Excel (Processed vs. Reduced).
- [ ] **Submission Prep:** Organize all `.ARFF/.CSV` files, models, and research articles.
- [ ] **Presentation:** 15-min live demo + 10-min Q&A. Ensure all members are camera-ready.

To score top marks, your literature review must reflect where the industry is going, not just where it has been.
